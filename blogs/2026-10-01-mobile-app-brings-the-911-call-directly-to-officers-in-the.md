When a call for service is dispatched, responders often approach the initial stages of a call with only a brief computer-aided dispatch (CAD) summary. While that information provides the essential details, it rarely captures the full context of the incident. Tone of voice, urgency, background activity and conversations between callers and telecommunicators can all influence how a responder approaches a scene.
