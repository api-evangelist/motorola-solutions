Intercom systems provide an extra layer of security to an apartment security system. This security technology allows residents to identify visitors and grant them access to the building, ensuring no unauthorized individuals can enter. Instead of needing to meet the visitor at the building’s entry point, intercom systems give tenants the ability to grant entry directly from inside their unit, adding convenience on top of safety.
