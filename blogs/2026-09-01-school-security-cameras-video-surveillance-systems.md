Creating a safe learning environment has become one of the highest priorities for schools across North America. From unauthorized visitors and vandalism to bullying, theft and emergency situations, today's educational institutions face a wide range of security challenges that require proactive planning rather than reactive responses. While no technology can prevent every incident, school security cameras have become an essential part of modern campus safety.
