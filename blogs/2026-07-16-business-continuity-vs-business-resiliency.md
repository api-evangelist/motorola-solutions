Streamlined communications, great customer or client outputs and positive working environments are essential parts of a company’s overall success. But long-term success is only possible if operations are maintained even as employees leave, management changes or the unexpected happens. A truly sustainable organization is one that creates emergency preparedness and response strategies to protect its operations, customers and employees from unexpected events.
