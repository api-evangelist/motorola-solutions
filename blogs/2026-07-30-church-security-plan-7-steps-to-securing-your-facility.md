Churches have long served as places of worship, community and refuge. While these spaces are designed to be welcoming and accessible, today’s security landscape requires churches to balance hospitality with preparedness. Whether your congregation has 100 members or several thousand, having a comprehensive church security plan helps reduce risks, improve emergency preparedness and protect people during everyday operations and unexpected events.
