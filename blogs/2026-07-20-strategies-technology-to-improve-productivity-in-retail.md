Retail store productivity is a balance between keeping store operations running smoothly, customers happy and employees motivated to perform well. The most productive retail stores are the ones that perfectly match their resources, including space, inventory and labor, to ‌customer demand without letting anything go to waste. This is seen in a store where everything runs smoothly, checkout lines move quickly, shelves are always stocked and employees can engage with and help customers.
